Privacy and terms

Last updated November 26, 2026

The short version: your plan is yours. We store only what's needed to sync it between your devices. We never sell it, never show ads, and you can delete it any time.

What we collect

  • Nothing, if you don't sign in. Your plan stays in your browser on that device.
  • If you sign in to sync: your email address, and your plan (course names, deadlines, steps you've ticked, marks you've entered, and any LEARN / D2L calendar link you connected).
  • If you send feedback: what you wrote, the email you chose to add (optional), and your browser type so we can reproduce bugs.
  • Basic, anonymous visit counts (pages viewed, device type), with no cookies and no tracking across sites.

What we don't collect

  • Your syllabus files. PDFs and photos are read on your own device and are never uploaded.
  • Your LEARN / D2L password. The calendar link only lets us read your calendar's dates.
  • Anything for advertising. There are no ads and no data brokers.

Where it lives

Synced plans are stored with Supabase (our database provider). The site is hosted by Vercel. When you connect a school calendar, our server fetches it for you and doesn't keep a copy beyond your plan.

Your choices

  • Delete your data: sign in, then press "Delete my data" at the bottom of the menu. Your synced plan is erased right away.
  • To remove your account email too, or ask what we hold about you, use "Send feedback" in the app and we'll handle it within 30 days.

We follow Canada's privacy law (PIPEDA). We only use your information to run StratAG.

Terms of use

  • StratAG is free and provided as is. It reads syllabi automatically and can make mistakes. Your course outline and your instructor are always the final word on dates and grades.
  • Grade estimates are a guide. Schools and courses weigh and round marks differently.
  • Don't misuse the service (no attacking it, scraping it, or using it to break your school's rules).
  • We may change or stop features. If we make a big change to this page, we'll say so in the app.

StratAG is made by Ikiloop in Kitchener-Waterloo, Ontario.
